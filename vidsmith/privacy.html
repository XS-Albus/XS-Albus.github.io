This policy covers Vidsmith for Android and iPhone, provided by Xiaoshan Chu. Contact: cxsandr@gmail.com.

Media and local data

Video editing and export happen on your device. Vidsmith does not upload your videos, photos or audio to developer-operated servers for processing. It accesses the media you select and creates local working files and exports. When you choose Share, the selected app can receive your exported file; that app's privacy practices apply. System photo or backup services may also handle your media under your own settings.

The app stores preferences, export counters and purchase-entitlement status locally. Temporary processing files are cleared as part of app cleanup. Saved gallery exports remain until you delete them using your device's controls. Removing the app does not necessarily remove files you saved to your gallery.

Usage analytics

When enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase processes app interactions, sessions, tool selections, export results and error categories, processing times, file sizes, paywall interactions and purchase-related events. This does not include media content. Automatic events include first-launch, screen-view and engagement information. The SDK also processes app-instance identifiers, device and app information, and approximate location derived from IP addresses. These identifiers can associate events with an app installation: the data should not be understood as completely anonymous.

We use analytics to understand product usage and improve reliability and usability. Vidsmith does not display advertising or use these events for advertising personalization. Google receives analytics data over encrypted connections. See Firebase privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy for its processing, including processing in countries other than your own.

Your analytics choice

Android 1.0 with the consent prompt: on a fresh installation, the app's analytics collection setting starts off. Selecting Allow analytics enables optional usage analytics; the app applies your choice through Firebase's collection and consent controls. Declining does not restrict app features, purchasing or restoration. You can change your choice in Settings.

Earlier Android test versions: analytics is enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings. This paragraph describes those older versions, not the fresh-install behavior of Android 1.0 with the consent prompt.

Current iPhone version: analytics is enabled by default. You can turn it off using the usage-data switch in Settings. This switch controls Analytics, not the separate Crashlytics and Remote Config services described below.

Turning the switch off disables optional analytics collection through Firebase's controls. It does not itself erase data already received by Google. Your choice does not disable the app store's own purchase processing or reporting.

iPhone crash reports and configuration

The iPhone app also uses Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and Remote Config to retrieve app configuration. These services can process installation identifiers, device/app information and, for Crashlytics, crash traces and diagnostic information. They operate separately from the analytics switch. The Android version described here does not include these two services.

Purchases

Apple or Google Play processes payments. The app uses purchase and entitlement information to unlock Pro and restore purchases, independently of analytics. We do not receive your payment-card details. Store transaction records are governed by the store's policies; disabling analytics or removing Vidsmith does not cancel a subscription.

Retention

Our Analytics property is configured to retain event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months. New activity resets the retention period for user identifiers. These settings do not generally limit standard aggregated reports and are not a promise that every record is removed on the same day.

For the iPhone services, Firebase states that Crashlytics keeps crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Remote Config installation identifiers are retained until a deletion request is made through the Firebase API. These are separate from Analytics retention settings.

Privacy requests

You may contact cxsandr@gmail.com to request information, access or deletion, or to raise a privacy concern. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to correction, objection, restriction, portability and to complain to a data-protection authority.

Vidsmith has no app account linking your email to an installation. We may need additional information to identify relevant records and verify a request; we will explain what can be located or deleted. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or private videos. Store-held records and data received by a sharing app may need to be requested from that provider. Emails you send us include your address and message and are used to respond to your inquiry. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the request, meet applicable legal obligations or resolve a dispute. The app has no account-deletion screen and no automatic tool for deleting Analytics records for an installation. An email address alone does not identify such records; if we cannot locate them, we will explain that limitation rather than claim they were deleted.

Children and policy changes

Vidsmith is not directed at children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information. Material changes to our practices will be reflected in this policy, and additional consent will be sought where required.

媒体与本机数据

视频编辑和导出在你的设备上完成。Vidsmith 不会把视频、照片或音频上传至开发者运营的服务器进行处理。 应用访问你选择的媒体，并在本机生成工作文件和导出文件。你主动使用“分享”时，所选应用可以接收导出文件， 其隐私规则适用于后续处理。系统相册或备份服务也可能根据你的设置处理媒体。

设置、导出计数和购买权益状态保存在本机。临时处理文件随应用清理流程清除；已保存到相册的导出文件由你通过设备管理， 卸载应用不一定会删除这些相册文件。

使用统计

开启统计后，Google Analytics for Firebase 处理应用互动、会话、工具选择、导出结果与错误分类、处理耗时、 文件大小、付费墙互动和购买相关事件，不包含媒体内容。自动事件包括首次打开、页面浏览和使用时长信息。 SDK 还处理应用实例标识、设备与应用信息，以及由 IP 地址推导的大致地区。 这些标识可以关联同一次应用安装的事件，因此不能理解为完全匿名。

我们使用统计了解产品使用情况，改进稳定性和易用性。Vidsmith 不展示广告，也不将这些事件用于广告个性化。 统计数据通过加密连接发送给 Google，可能在你所在国家以外处理。详情见 Firebase 隐私说明和 Google 隐私政策。

你的统计选择

带有首次询问的 Android 1.0：全新安装时，应用的统计采集设置默认关闭。 选择“允许统计”后启用可选的使用统计；应用通过 Firebase 的采集和同意控制应用你的选择。 拒绝不限制应用功能、购买或恢复权益，可随时在设置中更改。

较早的 Android 测试版本：统计默认开启，可在设置中关闭。 此段描述旧测试版本，不代表带有首次询问的 Android 1.0 全新安装行为。

当前 iPhone 版本：统计默认开启，可通过设置中的使用数据开关关闭。 该开关仅控制 Analytics，不控制下述独立的 Crashlytics 与 Remote Config 服务。

关闭开关会通过 Firebase 的控制关闭可选统计采集，不等于删除 Google 已接收的数据；也不会关闭应用商店自身的交易处理或报表。

iPhone 崩溃诊断与远程配置

iPhone 应用另外使用 Firebase Crashlytics 诊断崩溃、Remote Config 获取应用配置，可能处理安装标识、设备和应用信息， 以及崩溃堆栈和诊断信息。这两项服务独立于统计开关。本文所述 Android 版本没有集成这两项服务。

购买

付款由 Apple 或 Google Play 处理。应用使用购买及权益信息解锁 Pro 和恢复购买，不依赖使用统计。 我们不接收支付卡详细资料。商店交易记录按商店政策处理；关闭统计或卸载 Vidsmith 不会取消订阅。

数据保留

当前 Analytics 属性的事件级数据保留时间为 2 个月，用户级数据为 14 个月；新活动会重置用户标识的保留期限。 这些设置通常不限制标准聚合报表，也不代表所有记录都在同一天删除。

针对 iPhone 的独立服务，Firebase 说明 Crashlytics 将崩溃堆栈及关联标识保留 90 天，随后开始从在线和备份系统删除。 Remote Config 安装标识保留至通过 Firebase API 发起删除。这些期限不受 Analytics 保留设置控制。
