TileMath Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Data stored on your device

Your calculation history and app settings (units, language, appearance, currency symbol) are saved only on your device. You can delete all history at any time with "Clear all" on the History screen. TileMath has no accounts and no servers of its own.

Advertising

TileMath shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device information such as your IP address, general device data, ad interaction data, and — only if you allow tracking — your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not collect or see this data ourselves; it is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. A list of Google's certified ad partners is available here.

Your choices

Tracking (iOS): when first asked, you can choose "Ask App Not to Track". You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, ads remain but are not personalized.

EEA/UK users: on first launch you will be asked for consent under GDPR. You can change your choice anytime via "Privacy options" in the app's Settings.

Children

TileMath is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g., GDPR in the EEA, CCPA in California), you may have rights to access or delete personal data. Ad-related data is processed by Google; see the links above. For anything else, contact us.
