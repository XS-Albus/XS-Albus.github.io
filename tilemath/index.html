TileMath

A tile calculator that speaks feet, inches, and sixteenths.

Stop converting 12′ 3-1/2″ into a decimal just to figure out how many tiles you need. TileMath has a purpose-built fraction keypad, so you type measurements the way they're written on the tape.

What it does

  • Tile count for any number of rooms or sections
  • Boxes to buy, rounded up to whole boxes
  • Cost estimate by tile or by box
  • Grout spacing included in the math
  • Waste allowance for straight, diagonal, and herringbone layouts
  • Metric mode — meters, centimeters, millimeters
Offline-first. The core calculator works fully offline with no account required. Your calculations stay on your device; the app shows a small banner ad. See the privacy policy.
