隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 15 日

今宜事顺不收集、不上传、不出售你的任何个人信息。

我们不做的事

  • 没有账号，不需要注册或登录。
  • 没有广告、统计分析、崩溃上报，也没有任何第三方追踪 SDK。
  • App 不向我们发送任何数据。我们没有接收 App 数据的服务器。

只存在你设备上的内容

  • 你的设置，比如界面语言、双语显示和通知开关。
  • 是否已解锁完整版的标记。
  • 供小组件读取的未来一年日历摘要。
  • 你加进日历或设了提醒的日子，用来显示「已加入」「已提醒」。

这些内容只保存在你的设备上，删除 App 即全部清除。如果你开启了系统备份，Apple 或 Google 可能按你的备份设置一并备份，这部分受它们的隐私政策约束。

购买

购买和恢复购买由 App Store 或 Google Play 完成。付款信息由 Apple 或 Google 收集和处理，我们接触不到。App 只在你的设备上读取商店返回的购买结果，用来判断是否解锁，不会发送给我们。Google Play 的结算组件还会向 Google 发送它自身的运行情况，例如调用是否成功、连接是否出错，用于改进结算服务，受 Google 隐私政策约束。除了这些与购买相关的通信，App 不联网。

系统权限

  • 日历：只在你选择写入日历时请求，只用来新建那一条日程。我们不读取你已有的日程。在 Android 上，App 需要读取日历账户列表，以便选择写入哪个日历。
  • 通知：用于节气、节日和择日提醒，全部在你的设备上安排。手机重启后，App 会在设备上重新安排这些提醒。
  • 照片：只在你把分享卡片存进相册时，由系统请求「仅添加」权限。App 看不到你的相册。

你可以随时在系统设置中关闭这些权限，关闭后其余功能照常使用。

分享

分享卡片是在你的设备上生成的图片，通过系统分享面板发出，发给谁由你决定。

本网页

本网页不使用 Cookie、统计分析或广告追踪。网页托管在 GitHub Pages，GitHub 为了安全和正常运行，可能记录访问者的 IP 地址等技术信息，受 GitHub 隐私声明约束。

儿童

今宜事顺不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

变更

政策变更时会更新本页和生效日期。如果将来加入任何会收集数据的功能，会提前在 App 内和本页说明。

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026

Jinyi does not collect, upload or sell any of your personal information.

What we don't do

  • There are no accounts. You never sign up or sign in.
  • There are no ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • The app sends no data to us. We have no server that receives data from the app.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, such as language, bilingual display and notification switches.
  • A flag recording whether you have unlocked the full version.
  • A one-year calendar summary that the widgets read.
  • The days you added to your calendar or set reminders for, so the app can show them as added.

All of this is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete the app. If you have system backup turned on, Apple or Google may include it in your backup under their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Purchases and restores are handled by the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google collects and processes your payment details, and we never see them. The app reads the purchase result on your device to decide whether to unlock, and never sends it to us. Google Play's billing component also reports its own operating status to Google, such as whether calls succeed or connections fail, to improve the billing service. Google handles this under its own privacy policy. Apart from these purchase-related connections, the app does not go online.

Permissions

  • Calendar: requested only when you choose to add a date to your calendar, and used only to create that event. We don't read your existing events. On Android, the app reads the list of calendar accounts so you can choose where the event goes.
  • Notifications: used for solar term, festival and date reminders, all scheduled on your device. After a restart, the app reschedules them on the device.
  • Photos: requested only when you save a share card to your photos, with add-only access. The app cannot see your photo library.

You can turn these off in system settings at any time. Everything else keeps working.

Sharing

Share cards are images created on your device and sent through the system share sheet to whoever you choose.

This page

This page uses no cookies, analytics or ad tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical information such as visitor IP addresses for security and reliability, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Jinyi is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If we ever add a feature that collects data, we will say so in the app and on this page first.
